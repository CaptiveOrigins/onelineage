# MyHeritage

Read 2026-11-10, from public pages only, with no sign-in. Terms and Conditions, last updated 14 October 2026.

| Capability | Verdict | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `read_own_matches` | unclear | Matches are part of the service, but "You must not copy or store electronically any part of the Website or its contents". |
| `automate_page_visits` | not-allowed | "The use of AI agents, bots, automated systems, or any other artificial intelligence technology … to access, scrape, crawl, harvest, or collect data from the Website or Service … is strictly prohibited." |
| `site_export` | allowed | The service exists in part "to allow you to download the DNA Results". Match-list export isn't covered by this verdict. |
| `write_labels` | not-allowed | "Running macros or scripts to automate activities on the Website … is strictly prohibited." |
| `official_api` | unclear | There's a partner program for tree matching, e.g. RootsMagic and Family Historian, but no public API terms. |
| `raw_upload` | unclear | Uploads of other companies' files aren't described in the terms. |

**Law enforcement:** "Any use of the DNA Services for law enforcement purposes, forensic examinations … is strictly prohibited, unless a court order is obtained."

**Notes:** Section 2.6.2, new in October 2026: "You must not facilitate access to your MyHeritage account by any AI Tool or third-party automated interface." The license is "solely for personal, non-commercial, and human use". This affects any AI feature that touches MyHeritage data.
