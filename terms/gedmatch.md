# GEDmatch

Read 2026-11-10, from public pages only, with no sign-in. Terms of Service, effective 22 November 2024.

| Capability | Verdict | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `read_own_matches` | unclear | Matches are shared with other users for genealogy, but the terms ban anything that "copies or stores any significant portion of the Content". |
| `automate_page_visits` | not-allowed | Users may not do anything that "'crawls,' 'scrapes,' or 'spiders' any page, data, or portion of or relating to the Site or Content (through use of manual or automated means)". |
| `site_export` | not-allowed | "No means are provided on the Site to make Raw DNA or other DNA data available for download." |
| `write_labels` | unclear | Processes that run "while you are not logged into the Site" are banned, and nothing else applies. |
| `official_api` | unclear | No API or developer program is mentioned. |
| `raw_upload` | allowed | "Raw DNA data files uploaded to GEDmatch.com ('Raw Data') remain the property of the person who uploaded it." Uploads are limited to your own DNA, or DNA you're authorized to upload. |

**Law enforcement:** Law-enforcement matching is part of the service, through GEDmatch PRO. Each user picks Opt-in, Opt-out, Personal Research or Private for their kit.

**Notes:** Use is limited to personal genealogy and to investigations by or for law enforcement. Academic and institutional research is prohibited.
