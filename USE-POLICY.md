# Who may use OneLineage

**Status: draft, 2026-11-10.** This policy was decided by the owner. The wording below still needs a legal review before the first release.

OneLineage is for people researching families: their own, or someone else's with that person's permission.

## Allowed

- Personal family research.
- Research for someone else, such as a relative, a friend, a client or an adoptee.
- Verifiable investigative genetic genealogy by an appropriately sanctioned lab.
- Wildlife agencies.

## Not allowed

- Any use by law enforcement, or on its behalf, except through an appropriately sanctioned lab as described above.

Exactly where some lines fall is still being settled. Examples are identifying unknown remains, and the scope for wildlife agencies. This file will say so when it's settled.

## How it's applied

- The license's Required Notice points here.
- The app asks once, on first launch, which kind of use it will be, and keeps the answer in your library.
- The app doesn't try to guess or watch how it's used.

A local app with no servers can't police its own use. This policy sets expectations, and gives grounds to act on a known breach.
